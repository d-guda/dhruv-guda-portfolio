# Sources and Research Provenance

Descriptions and citations retain the original research trail with repaired URLs and added methodology/control references. Historical architecture, company-profile details, and incident snapshots are dated context; not every external page was independently reverified. Community posts are anecdotal leads, not confirmed company findings.

1. **Microsoft News Center — Company identity:** [Microsoft News Center](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2014/09/15/minecraft-to-join-microsoft/). Mojang AB (Mojang Studios) is the Stockholm-based developer of Minecraft. Microsoft announced its $2.5 billion acquisition of Mojang in 2014, and Mojang joined Microsoft Studios (now Xbox Game Studios).
2. **Microsoft Services Agreement — Business model:** [Microsoft Services Agreement](https://www.microsoft.com/en/servicesagreement). Video-game and entertainment business. Revenue paths include sales and licensing of games and digital content, subscriptions, in-game/Marketplace purchases, and distribution through the wider Xbox ecosystem.
3. **Minecraft Realms — Business model:** [Minecraft Realms](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/realms). Minecraft Realms is a recurring hosted personal-server subscription. Plan prices vary by edition, region, and date; current prices are not asserted in this assessment.
4. **Minecraft Partner Program — Business model:** [Minecraft Partner Program](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/partner). Minecraft Marketplace sells creator-made digital content through Minecoins. Mojang also operates a creator partner program, and Marketplace Pass/Realms Plus provide subscription access to rotating content.
5. **Microsoft acquisition announcement — Critical assets:** [Microsoft acquisition announcement](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2014/09/15/minecraft-to-join-microsoft/). Minecraft intellectual property: trademarks, game code, art, world formats, proprietary development assets, and the Minecraft brand/franchise.
6. **Microsoft Privacy Statement — Critical assets:** [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement). Player and customer data: names/contact details, email addresses, account credentials, payment data, subscription/license entitlements, purchase history, IP addresses, device identifiers, and connectivity/configuration data.
7. **Minecraft account guide — Critical assets:** [Minecraft account guide](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/how-create-minecraft-account). Account identity and access data: Microsoft account credentials, Xbox gamertags, purchase entitlements, multiplayer permissions, and account security settings.
8. **Minecraft Help - Realms backups — Critical assets:** [Minecraft Help - Realms backups](https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/28717462139149). Player-created content and service data: Realm worlds, saves, backups, in-game content, and social/usage data associated with online play.
9. **AWS News Blog - Hosting Minecraft Realms on AWS — Critical assets:** [AWS News Blog - Hosting Minecraft Realms on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/hosting-minecraft-realms-on-aws/). Candidate assets include authentication, hosting, storage, networking, monitoring, and recovery systems. The linked 2014 deployment is historical evidence, not proof of current architecture.
10. **Minecraft Partner Program — Critical assets:** [Minecraft Partner Program](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/partner). Marketplace creator content, partner submissions, Minecoin transactions, and the trust/safety processes used to review published content.
11. **Mojang Studios LinkedIn company page — Operations:** [Mojang Studios LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mojangstudios). Historical public company-profile research described a distributed workforce and multiple locations. This is not a verified current office inventory.
12. **Minecraft Realms — Operations:** [Minecraft Realms](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/realms). Minecraft and Realms operate across PCs, consoles, and mobile devices. Bedrock supports cross-platform play across Windows, Android, iOS, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Amazon Fire, and Chromebook; Java supports Windows, macOS, and Linux.
13. **AWS News Blog - Hosting Minecraft Realms on AWS — Operations:** [AWS News Blog - Hosting Minecraft Realms on AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/hosting-minecraft-realms-on-aws/). A publicly documented 2014 Realms deployment used AWS EC2, EBS, RDS, S3, Elastic Load Balancing, and multiple AWS Regions. Worlds and game state were stored in S3 at that time.
14. **Guinness World Records — Scale:** [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/best-selling-video-game). Minecraft had sold more than 350 million units as of April 2025, according to Guinness World Records, which says the figure was confirmed by Mojang in the official Minecraft Annual 2026.
15. **Microsoft FY2025 Q4 earnings call — Scale:** [Microsoft FY2025 Q4 earnings call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2025/earnings-fy-2025-q4). Microsoft reported that Minecraft reached record monthly active usage and revenue in FY2025 Q4, but it did not publish a current Minecraft-specific monthly-active-user number in that disclosure.
16. **Microsoft Security Response Center — Recent incident:** [Microsoft Security Response Center](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/msrc/blog/2021/12/microsofts-response-to-cve-2021-44228-apache-log4j2). Log4Shell / CVE-2021-44228 affected Minecraft: Java Edition in December 2021. Mojang patched official clients and Microsoft released a fix blocking the issue in Java Edition 1.18.1; self-hosted server operators also had to update or apply mitigations.
17. **CurseForge Support — Recent incident:** [CurseForge Support](https://support.curseforge.com/support/solutions/articles/9000228509-june-2023-infected-mods-detection-tool). In June 2023, malware was distributed through infected Minecraft mods/projects on CurseForge and BukkitDev. This was a third-party mod ecosystem incident, not evidence that Mojang's own network was breached.
18. **BleepingComputer — Recent incident:** [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-fractureiser-malware-used-curseforge-minecraft-mods-to-infect-windows-linux/). Independent security reporting described the 2023 Fractureiser malware as an information stealer spread through compromised Minecraft mods and plugins.
19. **Microsoft Security Response Center — Recent incident:** [Microsoft Security Response Center](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/msrc/blog/2024/01/microsoft-actions-following-attack-by-nation-state-actor-midnight-blizzard). In January 2024, parent company Microsoft disclosed that Midnight Blizzard accessed a small percentage of corporate email accounts after password-spraying a legacy test tenant. Microsoft stated there was no evidence of access to customer environments, production systems, source code, or AI systems.
20. **Minecraft official site — Official website:** [Minecraft official site](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us). Minecraft's official website is Minecraft.net. Mojang's official careers page is hosted within Minecraft.net and identifies Mojang AB / Microsoft branding.
21. **Microsoft 2025 Annual Report — Financial filings:** [Microsoft 2025 Annual Report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar25/index.html). Mojang Studios is not separately publicly traded and does not publish its own U.S. Form 10-K. Use parent company Microsoft Corporation's FY2025 Form 10-K for public financial and enterprise-risk disclosures; Microsoft reports gaming within its consolidated business.
22. **SpigotMC community forum — Industry forum:** [SpigotMC community forum](https://www.spigotmc.org/threads/minecraft-security-bonus-part-1-log4j.538069/). SpigotMC's Systems Administration forum contains practitioner discussion of Minecraft server security and Log4j.
23. **Microsoft Q&A — Industry forum:** [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/4103894/minecraft-account-hacked-and-email-taken-off-micro). Microsoft Q&A contains public discussions of compromised Microsoft/Minecraft accounts and recovery problems.
24. **CISA Joint Cybersecurity Advisory AA21-356A — Cybersecurity report:** [CISA Joint Cybersecurity Advisory AA21-356A](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa21-356a). CISA, FBI, NSA, and international partners published mitigation guidance for Log4Shell and related Log4j vulnerabilities.
25. **NIST NVD - CVE-2021-44228 — Cybersecurity report:** [NIST NVD - CVE-2021-44228](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2021-44228). NIST's National Vulnerability Database entry for CVE-2021-44228 documents the affected Log4j versions and technical vulnerability record.
26. **Microsoft Security Response Center — Incident update:** [Microsoft Security Response Center](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/msrc/blog/2024/03/update-on-microsoft-actions-following-attack-by-nation-state-actor-midnight-blizzard). Microsoft's March 8, 2024 update reported access to some source repositories and internal systems following the earlier corporate-email compromise. This parent-company incident does not establish a Mojang breach.
27. **Joint Task Force Transformation Initiative — Methodology:** [Joint Task Force Transformation Initiative](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/30/r1/final). NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 informs assessment concepts; the 4 x 4 scores, bands, costs, owners, and residual estimates here are project-defined assumptions.
28. **OWASP Password Storage Cheat Sheet — Control guidance:** [OWASP Password Storage Cheat Sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html). Password verifiers require appropriate salted password hashing rather than reversible password encryption. Other recoverable sensitive data requires separate encryption and access controls.

The original malformed CurseForge citation was corrected to the provider’s verified article URL. Microsoft’s March 2024 update is included alongside the earlier January statement.
